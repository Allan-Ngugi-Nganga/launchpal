Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

LaunchPal is a Chrome extension that audits websites. This page says exactly what it touches and what leaves your browser. It applies to the extension and to this website.

The short version

Scans happen inside your browser. Page content, page source, found secrets, URLs and findings never leave your machine. The only network call the extension makes is a licence check, which sends a licence key and a random install id.

What is stored on your device

Kept in chrome.storage and never transmitted:

  • the name you give your blob, synced with your Chrome profile
  • your licence key, plan, expiry and a random install id
  • the origins you have verified, and the verification token for each
  • for Pro users only: saved scan history and findings

Uninstalling the extension removes all of it.

What leaves your browser

  • Licence checks. On startup and when you activate a key, the extension sends your licence key and install id to our licence service. Nothing else. No page data, no URLs, no findings.
  • Payments. Card, M-Pesa, Airtel Money and bank transfers are handled by Paystack on its own hosted checkout page. Paystack is the payment processor and holds your payment details. We receive the payment event and the email address used at checkout. That address is what a licence is tied to.
  • Key delivery. After payment, Paystack returns you to our claim page, which checks the transaction with Paystack and shows your licence key once. Only a hash of the key is stored, so it cannot be shown again. We do not store the key itself.

Nothing else is collected. No analytics, no advertising, no telemetry, no crash reporting.

What a scan reads

  • With the page you have open, after you click the extension icon: the rendered page, its scripts, forms, links, images, styles and response headers.
  • For deeper scans only, on origins you verify by publishing a token: read-only GET and HEAD requests to that same origin, rate limited, at most 40 per scan, stoppable at any time.

Deeper scans never run on a site you have not verified.

Secrets

If the scan finds something that looks like a credential in page source, the value is masked in the report, for example sk-...a3f9. Full secrets are never displayed, never stored, and never sent anywhere.

This website

The site is static. It sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, and uses no analytics. Server logs at our host record the usual request metadata (IP address, user agent, requested path) for security and abuse prevention.

Your choices

  • Deactivate a browser in Settings to release an activation slot.
  • Delete saved scans in the extension, or uninstall to remove everything local.
  • Ask us to delete the licence record tied to your email address by writing to maverick.develops@gmail.com.

Children

LaunchPal is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the Chrome Web Store listing is updated to match.
